magic loop
Round 1
ch3(count as dc), 15dc in the loop, sl st to the top of ch3, fasten off.

Round 2
New yarn in any st, ch3(count as dc)+dc, third loop only(TLO): dc inc, dc, dc inc in next st, [dc inc in next st, TLO: dc inc, dc, dc inc in next st] repeat 3 times, sl st to the top of the first ch3, fasten off.

Round 3
New yarn in the left dc of the dc inc located at the right end of one side, ch3(count as dc)+dc, TLO: dc inc, 3dc, dc inc, and dc inc in next st, [dc inc in next st, TLO: dc inc, 3dc, dc inc, and dc inc in next st] repeat 3 times, sl st to the top of the first ch3, fasten off.

Round 4
New yarn in the right dc of the dc inc located at the right end of one side, ch3(count as dc)+dc, TLO: dc inc, 7dc, dc inc, and dc inc in next st, [dc inc in next st, TLO: dc inc, 7dc, dc inc, and dc inc in next st] repeat 3 times, sl st to the top of the ch3, fasten off.

Round 5
New yarn in the right dc of the dc inc located at the right end of one side, ch3(count as dc)+dc, TLO: dc inc, 11dc, dc inc, and dc inc in next st, [dc inc in next st, TLO: dc inc, 11dc, dc inc, and dc inc in next st] repeat 3 times, sl st to the top of the first ch3, fasten off.

Round 6
New yarn in the gap between any two of the dc inc groups, ch1+sc, TLO: 18sc, sc+ch2 in next gap, [sc in the same gap, TLO: 18sc, sc+ch2 in next gap] repeat 3 times, sl st to the first sc.

Round 7
ch1+sc, sc in following sts, 4sc in next corner, [sc in following sts, 4sc in corner] repeat 3 times, sl st to the first sc, fasten off.
