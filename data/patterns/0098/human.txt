Make a magic ring.
Round 1: Ch 3, work 2 tr into ring, * ch 2, work 3 tr into ring, rep from * twice, ch 2, join with a sl st into top of beg ch-3.
Sl st across the next 2 stitches and into the corner sp. Fasten off.
Round 2: Join new colour in any ch-2 corner sp, ch 3, work 2 tr into corner sp, ch 2, work 3 tr into same sp, * ch 1, work 3 tr, ch 2, 3 tr into next corner sp, rep from * twice, ch 1, join with a sl st into top of beg ch-3.
Sl st across the next 2 stitches and into the corner sp. Fasten off.
Round 3: Join new colour in any ch-2 corner sp, ch 3, work 2 tr into corner sp, ch 2, work 3 tr into same sp, * ch 1, work 3 tr into ch-1 sp, ch 1, work 3 tr, ch 2, 3 tr into next corner sp, rep from * twice, ch 1, work 3 tr into ch-1 sp, ch 1, join with a sl st into top of beg ch-3.
Sl st across the next 2 stitches and into the corner sp. Fasten off.

Sl st across the next 2 stitches and into the corner sp. Fasten off.
Round 5: Join new colour in any ch-2 corner sp, ch 3, work 2 tr into corner sp, ch 2, work 3 tr into same sp, * ch 1, work 3 tr into ch-1 sp, ch 1, work 3 tr into next ch-1 sp, ch 1, work 3 tr into next ch-1 sp, ch 1, work 3 tr, ch 2, 3 tr into next corner sp, rep from * twice, ch 1, work 3 tr into ch-1 sp, ch 1, work 3 tr into next ch-1 sp, ch 1, work 3 tr into next ch-1 sp, ch 1, join with a sl st into top of beg ch-3.
Sl st across the next 2 stitches and into the corner sp. Fasten off.
