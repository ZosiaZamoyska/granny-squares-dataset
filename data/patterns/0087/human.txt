Abbreviations (US Terms):
Ch - Chain
Dc -Double Crochet
Sk - Skip
Sl st - Slip Stitch
Sp - Space

Important Notes:
-3 dc = dc cluster
-All joins are made with a sl st.
-These brackets [ ] indicate a group of stitches that all need to be worked in the same space.
-The beginning Ch 2 of each row counts as a dc.

Make a magic ring.

Round 1: ch 2, 2 dc in circle, ch 2. *3 dc in circle, ch 2.* Repeat *to* 2 more times. Pull circle tight and join. (12 dc)

Round 2: Sl st over next sp, sl st into ch sp. [Ch 2, 2 dc, ch 2, 3 dc] all in ch 2 space. *Sk 3 dc, in next ch sp: [3 dc, ch 2, 3dc].* Repeat *to* 2 more times. Join. (24 dc)

Round 3: Sl st over next sp, sl st into ch sp. [Ch 2, 2 dc, ch 2, 3 dc] all in ch 2 space. Sk 3 dc, 3 dc between dc clusters. *Sk 3 dc, [3 dc, ch 2, 3dc] all in next ch 2 sp. Sk 3 dc, 3 dc between dc clusters.* Repeat *to* 2 more times. Join. (36 dc)

Now, you can continue on for as many rows as you would like, working [3 dc, ch 2, 3 dc] in each corner space & working 3 dc between each dc clusters.
