ABBREVIATIONS / REFERENCES
beg = begin(s)(ning)
ch(s) = chain(s)
ch-space = space previously made
dc = double crochet
rep = repeat(s)(ing)
rnd(s) = round(s)
sc = single crochet
sk = skip
sl = slip
sp(s) = space(s)
st(s) = stitch(es)
tr = treble (triple) crochet

Make a magic ring.
Round 1: Ch 1, work 8 sc in ring, join with sl st in beg sc.
Round 2: Ch 4 (counts as dc and ch-1), (dc in next sc, ch 1) around, join with sl st in 3rd ch of beg ch-4 – 8 ch-1 sps.
Round 3: Ch 3 (counts as dc), 3 dc in next ch-1 sp, (dc in next dc, 3 dc in next ch-1 sp) around, join with sl st in top of beg ch – 32 dc.
Round 4: Ch 9 (counts as tr and ch-5), tr in same st, [sk 3 dc, (3 tr, ch 5, 3 tr) in next dc, sk 3 dc, * (tr, ch 5, tr) in next dc] around, end last rep at *, join with sl st in 4th ch of beg ch-9.
Round 5: Sl st into 2nd ch of next ch-5 sp, ch 1, sc in same ch-5 sp, [ch 7, (3 tr, ch 5, 3 tr) in next ch-5 sp, ch 7, * sc in next ch-5 sp] around, end last rep at *, join with sl st in beg sc. Fasten off.
