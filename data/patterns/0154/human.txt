ch – chain
dc – double crochet
sl-st – slip stitch
sp – space
rep – repeat
st(s) – stitch(es)
Make a magic ring.
Round 1: (Yarn A, Pink) Ch 3 (counts as 1 dc), 3 dc into ring, ch 1, *(4 dc into ring, ch 1) — repeat from * 3 more times (4 groups total). Join with sl st to the top of the beginning ch-3. Break off Yarn A.
Round 2: (Yarn B, Yellow) Join Yarn B in any ch-1 space, ch 3 (counts as 1 dc), In the same space: 3 dc, ch 1, 4 dc (corner made), Ch 1, *(4 dc, ch 1, 4 dc) into next ch-1 space, ch 1; repeat from * three times more. Join with a sl st to the top of the beginning ch-3. Break off Yarn B.
Round 3: (Yarn C, White) Join Yarn C between any corner space, ch 3 (counts as 1 dc), ch 2, 1 dc in the same corner space. *1 dc in each dc across to the next corner, including 1 dc in the side ch-1 space. In each corner ch-1 space, work: (1 dc, ch 2, 1 dc). Rep from * around. Join with a sl st to the top of the beginning ch-3. Break off Yarn C.
Round 4: (Yarn D, Turquoise) Join Yarn D in any corner space between the 2 dc's, ch 3 (counts as one dc), In the same corner space: 3 dc, ch 1, 4 dc (corner made), ch 1, *Skip 4 dc, 2 dc in next dc, ch 1, 2 dc in next dc, ch 1, skip 4 dc, (4 dc, ch 1, 4 dc) in next corner, ch 1; repeat from * around. Join with a sl st to the top of the beginning ch-3. Break off Yarn D.
Round 5: (Yarn E, Lime Green) Join Yarn E in any corner space, ch 3 (counts as one dc), 1 dc, ch 2, 2 dc in same corner space (one corner made). Work 1 dc in each dc and 1 dc in each ch-1 space across to the next corner. In each corner space, work: *(2 dc, ch 2, 2 dc). Work 1 dc in each dc and 1 dc in each ch-1 space across to the next corner: rep from * around. Join with a sl st to the top of the beginning ch-3. Break off Yarn E.
