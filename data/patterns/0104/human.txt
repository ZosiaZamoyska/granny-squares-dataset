USA STITCH TERMS
Abbreviations:
· Ch = Chain
· SC = Single crochet
· DC = Double crochet
· Rep = Repeat
· St = Stitch
· Ch sp = Chain space
Standing Double Crochet: Yarn over, insert your hook into the stitch, yarn over, pull up a loop so that there are three loops on the hook. Yarn over and pull through two loops, yarn over, and pull through the remaining loops.

Make a magic ring.
Round 1: Ch 5 (counts as 1 DC and ch 2) and rep *3 DC, ch 2* three times in the magic ring. Make 2 DC and sl st to join. Fasten off and cut the yarn. Tighten the magic ring by pulling the yarn tail. Make 3 more of these mini granny squares in the colours of your choice. Grab one of the granny squares and attach the yarn to any corner ch sp with 1 standing DC.
Round 2: Make (2 DC, ch 2, 3 DC) in the same space. Make (3 DC, ch 2, 3 DC) in each ch sp across. At the end of the round, sl st to join.
