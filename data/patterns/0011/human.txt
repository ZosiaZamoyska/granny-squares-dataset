ch– chain stitch
ch-sp – chain space
dc– double crochet
rep– repeat
st– stitch
slst– slip stitch for joining for different round.

magic ring

Round 1
ch3(count as dc)+2dc in same ch-sp, ch3, [3dc in next ch-sp, ch3] rep 3 times, slst to top of ch3. 
Round 2
ch3 (count as dc), 2dc in same ch-sp, ch1, 3dc in next corner sp, ch3, [3dc in same corner sp, ch3, 3dc in next corner sp, ch3] rep 3 times, slst to top of ch3. 

Round 3
All sts will be in ch-sp: ch3(count as dc), 2dc in same ch-sp, ch1
switch blue yarn: 3dc, ch1
switch pink yarn: (3dc+ch3+3dc) in next corner sp, ch1, 3dc 
switch blue yarn:(3dc+2dc+ch3+3dc) in next corner sp, ch1
switch pink yarn: 3dc, ch1
switch blue yarn: (3dc+ch3+3dc) in next corner sp, ch1
switch pink yarn: 3dc, ch, 3dc in next corner sp, ch3, slst to top of ch3, fasten off.

Round 4
Join new color yarn in corner sp, ch3(count as dc), (2dc+ch3+3dc) in same corner sp, **[ch1, *(3dc in next ch-sp, ch) rep* 2 times]**, [(3dc+ch3+3dc) in next corner sp, rep from**to**] rep 3 times, slst to top of ch3, fasten off and weave in ends.
