ch– chain stitch
ch-sp – chain space
dc– double crochet
dtr– double treble crochet
FPdtr– front post double treble crochet
rep– repeat
st– stitch
slst– slip stitch for joining for different round.


Magic ring,
Round 1 
ch3=dc 2dc, ch2, [3dc, ch2] rep 13 times, tighten tail, slst to top, fasten off.

Round 2
Join new color yarn in any ch-sp, (an ch3=dc+2dc+ch2+3dc) in same ch-sp, ch1, [(3dc+2dc+ch2+3dc) in next ch-sp, ch1] rep 3 times, slst to top, fasten off.

Round 3
Join new color yarn in any corner sp, (an 3ch=dc+2dc+ch2+3dc) in same ch-sp, ch1, 3dc in next ch-sp, ch1, [(3dc+2dc+ch2+3dc) in next ch-sp, ch1, 3dc in next ch-sp, ch1] rep 3 times, slst to top, fasten off.

Round 4
Join new color yarn in any corner sp, note: in this round we make all dc in ch-sp:
(an 3ch=dc+2dc+ch2+3dc) in same ch-sp, ch1, [3dc, ch1] rep 2 times, [(3dc+2dc+ch2+3dc) in next ch-sp, ch1, *(3dc, ch1) rep* 2 times] rep 3 times, slst to top, fasten off.

Round 5
Join new color yarn in corner sp, note: make all dc in ch-sp:

(3ch=dc+2dc+ch2+3dc) in same ch-sp, ch1, ch1, dc, In round 2: (FPdtr in 2nd shell's st, FPdtr in 1st shell's 3ch=dc), ch1, 3dc, ch1, 

[(3dc+2dc+ch2+3dc) in next corner sp, ch1, 3dc, ch1, dc, In round 2: (FPdtr in 2nd shell's st 3ch=dc, FPdtr in 1st shell's last 3ch=dc), ch1, 3dc, ch1], slst to top , fasten off and weave in ends.
