Start with a magic ring, also called magic circle.
Round 1: ch4 (=1dc,1ch), 1dc + 1ch 11 times into circle. Join with a ss in 3rd ch. Pull on free end to tighten magic circle. 12dc total.
Round 2: Start in a ch 1 space. Attach new color as desired. ch 3 (=1dc), 1dc, ch1, *2dc, ch1* in remaining spaces. Join with ss in 3rd ch. 12 groups of 2dc.
Round 3: Start in a ch 1 space. Attach new color as desired. ch 3 (=1dc), 2dc, ch1, *3dc, ch1* in remaining spaces. Join with ss in 3rd ch. 12 groups of 3dc.
Round 4: Start in a ch 1 space. Attach new color as desired. ch 4 (= 1 tr), 1tr, 3ch, 2 tr (first corner) *ch 1, (4dc) in next ch space, 1 ch (4dc) in next space, 1 ch (2tr, ch3, 3tr) (=2nd corner). Join with ss in top chain, trim yarn and fasten off.
