ch – chain
sl st – slip stitch
dc – double crochet
sp – space
st – stitch

Make a magic ring.
Round 1: Ch 3 (counts as 1 dc), 2 dc into ring, ch 2.
3 dc into ring, ch 2 – repeat 3 more times (4 clusters total).
Sl st into top of starting ch 3. Fasten off.
Round 2: (Color B):
Join yarn in any ch-2 sp.
Ch 3, 2 dc in same sp, ch 2, 3 dc in same sp (1 corner made).
In next ch-2 sp: 3 dc, ch 2, 3 dc – repeat 3 more times.
Sl st to top of ch 3. Fasten off.
Round 3: (Color C):
Join in any ch-2 corner sp.
Ch 3, 2 dc in corner sp, ch 2, 3 dc in same sp.
In next sp between clusters: 3 dc.
Corner sp: 3 dc, ch 2, 3 dc; Side sp: 3 dc – repeat around.
Sl st to join. Fasten off.
Round 4: (Color D):
Repeat same pattern:
Corners: (3 dc, ch 2, 3 dc)
Sides: (3 dc in each sp between clusters)
Fasten off and weave in ends.
Rounds 5-8: Repeat using Colors E, A, B, and D respectively.
Continue working 3 dc clusters in each sp between clusters, and 3 dc, ch 2, 3 dc in corner sps.
