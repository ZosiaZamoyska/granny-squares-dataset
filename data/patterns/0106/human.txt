USA STITCH TERMS
Abbreviations:
· Ch = Chain
· Inc = Increase
· SC = Single crochet
· HDC = Half double crochet
· DC = Double crochet
· Rep = Repeat
· St = Stitch
· Ch sp = Chain space

Make a magic ring.
Round 1: Ch 3 (counts as 1 DC) and make 9 DC in the ring. Sl st to join. (10) Pull the tail to close the ring.
Round 2: Ch 2 and make 1 DC in the same st. Rep *2DC inc* in each st across. Sl st to join. (20)
Round 3: Ch 2 and make 1 DC in the same st. Rep *1 DC, 2DC inc* across. Sl st to join. (30)
Round 4: Ch 2 and make 1 DC in the same st. Rep *2 DC, 2DC inc* across. Sl st to join. (40) Fasten off and cut the yarn. Attach the second colour.
Round 5: Make (1 DC, ch 2, 2 DC) in the same st. Rep *1 DC, 1 HDC, 5 SC, 1 HDC, 1 DC, and (2 DC, ch 2, 2 DC)* three times. For the last side, make 1 DC, 1 HDC, 5 SC, 1 HDC, and 1 DC. Sl st to join.
Round 6: Ch 1 and make 1 HDC in the same st and each st across. For every corner ch 2 sp, make (2 HDC, ch 2, 2 HDC). At the end of the round, sl st to join.
Round 7: Ch 1 and make 1 SC in the same space and each st across. For each corner ch sp, make (1 SC, ch 2, 1 SC). At the end of the round, sl st to join. Fasten off and cut the yarn. Weave in the ends.
