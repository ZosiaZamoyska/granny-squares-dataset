Notes:
- ch 2 at beginning of rnds 1 & 2 do not count as a stitch.
Make a magic ring.
Round 1: Ch 3, 12 dc in 3rd ch from hook. Join with a sl st to 1st dc. (12 dc)
Round 2: Ch 2, 2 dc in each dc around. Join as before. (24 dc)
Round 3: Ch 5, dc in same dc as joining, skip next dc. *(dc, ch 2, dc) in next dc, skip next dc. Repeat from * around. Join with a sl st to 3rd ch of beginning ch 5.
Round 4: Sl st into ch 2 space, (ch 3, 2 dc, ch 2, 3 dc) in same space. *{ch 2, sc in next ch 2 space}, 2 times. Ch 2, (3 dc, ch 2, 3 dc) in next ch 2 space. Repeat from * 2 more times. {ch 2, sc in next ch 2 space} 2 times. Ch 2, join with a sl st to top of ch 3.
Round 5: Sl st over to ch 2 corner space, (ch 3, 2 dc, ch 2, 3 dc) in same space. *ch 2, 3 dc in each of the next 3 ch 2 spaces. Ch 2, (3 dc, ch 2, 3 dc) in next ch 2 corner space. Repeat from * 2 more times. Ch 2, 3 dc in each of the next 3 ch 2 spaces. Ch 2 , join as before.
Round 6: Sl st over to ch 2 corner space, (ch 3, 2 dc, ch 2, 3 dc) in same space. *ch 1, 2 dc in ch 2 space, dc in each of next 9 dc, 2 dc in ch 2 space, ch 1. (3 dc, ch 2, 3 dc) in next ch 2 corner space. Repeat from * 2 more times. Ch 1, 2 dc in ch 2 space, dc in each of next 9 dc, 2 dc in ch 2 space, ch 1. Join as before. End off.
