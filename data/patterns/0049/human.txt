You will need just a few stitches:
Chain – making a simple loop
Slip stitch – joining to a stitch without forming a new stitch
Double crochet (US) – a simple stitch

Make a magic ring.
Round 1: Chain 3 (as the first double crochet). Make two further double crochets into the center of the chain loop. Chain two (this will form the first corner). Make three double crochets into the chain loop. Chain two (this is your second corner). Make three double crochets into the chain loop. Chain two (this is your third corner). Make three double crochets into the chain loop. Chain two, then slip stitch into the third chain of your chain 3.
Round 2: Begin in a new color or the same color as you prefer, working from the corner you left the loose end in. Chain 3 (as the first double crochet), then make 2 double crochets next to it. Chain 2 (your first corner of this round). Make three double crochets into the same corner gap. Chain 1. Into the next corner make three double crochets, then chain two, then make three further double crochets. Chain 1. Into the next corner make three double crochets, then chain two, then make three further double crochets. Chain 1. Into the next corner make three double crochets, then chain two, then make three further double crochets. Chain 1 then slip stitch into the top of your chain 3.
Round 3: Begin in a new color or the same color as you prefer, working from the corner you left the loose end in. Chain 3 (as the first double crochet), then make 2 double crochets next to it. Chain 2. Make three double crochets into the same corner gap. Chain 1. Make three double crochets into the edge gap. Chain 1. Into the next corner make three double crochets, then chain two, then make three further double crochets. Chain 1. Make three double crochets into the edge gap. Chain 1. Into the next corner make three double crochets, then chain two, then make three further double crochets. Chain 1. Make three double crochets into the edge gap. Chain 1. Into the next corner make three double crochets, then chain two, then make three further double crochets. Chain 1. Make three double crochets into the edge gap. Chain 1 then then slip stitch into the top of your chain 3.
Round 4: Begin in a new color or the same color as you prefer, working from the corner you left the loose end in. Chain 3 (as the first double crochet), then make 2 double crochets next to it. Chain 2. Make three double crochets into the same corner gap. Chain 1. Make three double crochets into the edge gap. Chain 1. Make three double crochets into the edge gap. Chain 1. Into the next corner make three double crochets, then chain two, then make three further double crochets. Chain 1. Make three double crochets into the edge gap. Chain 1. Make three double crochets into the edge gap. Chain 1. Into the next corner make three double crochets, then chain two, then make three further double crochets. Chain 1. Make three double crochets into the edge gap. Chain 1. Make three double crochets into the edge gap. Chain 1. Into the next corner make three double crochets, then chain two, then make three further double crochets. Chain 1. Make three double crochets into the edge gap. Chain 1. Make three double crochets into the edge gap. Chain 1 then slip stitch into the top of the chain three.
