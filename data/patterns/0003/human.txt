Make a magic ring
Alternatively, you can make a magic ring.
Round 1: 3 ch (counts as tr here and throughout), into ring work 2 tr, dtr, [3 tr, dtr] three times, join with ss into top of 3-ch. (16 sts.)
Round 2: 3 ch, tr in next 2 sts, *[tr, dtr, tr] in next dtr, tr in next 3 sts, rep from * twice more, [tr, dtr, tr] in last dtr, join with ss into top of 3-ch. (24 sts.)
Round 3: 3 ch, tr in next 3 sts, *[2 tr, dtr, 2 tr] in next dtr, tr in next 5 sts, rep from * twice more, [2 tr, dtr, 2 tr] in next st, tr in last st, join with ss into top of 3-ch. (40 sts.)
Round 4: 3 ch, *tr in each st to corner dtr, [2 tr, dtr, 2 tr] in corner dtr, rep from * three more times, tr in each st to end, join with ss into top of 3 ch. (56 sts.)
