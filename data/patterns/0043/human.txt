All tutorials are written in UK terms.

Make a magic ring.
Round 1: 4ch, *3tr into loop, 2ch, repeat from * twice more, 2tr into loop, ss into 3rd chain of beginning 4ch.
For each new round...
join new yarn to corner space, or if using the same colour, ss into corner space and then:
Round 2: 4ch, 2tr in same sp, *1tr in each tr, (2tr, 2ch, 2tr) in corner sp, repeat from * twice more, 1tr in each tr, 1tr in beginning corner sp, ss to 3rd chain of beginning 4ch.
All subsequent Rounds, as R2.
