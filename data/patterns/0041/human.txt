Terms & Abbreviations
Chain(s): ch(s)
Slip Stitch(s): ss(s)
Double Crochet(s): dc(s)
Treble Crochet(s): tr(s)
You are viewing this pattern in UK terms.

Make a magic ring.
Round 1: 3 ch (counts as tr here and throughout), into ring work 2 tr, 3 ch, *3 tr, 3 ch, repeat from * twice more, join with ss into top of 3-ch. Fasten off. (12 tr, 4 3-ch sps)
Round 2: 1 ch (does not count as st here or throughout), [dc, 3 ch, dc] in same sp, *2 ch, [dc, 3 ch, dc] in next 3-ch sp, repeat from * twice more, 2 ch, join with ss into top of dc. Fasten off. (8 dc, 4 2-ch sps, 4 3-ch sps)
Round 3: 3 ch, work [2 tr, 3 ch, 3 tr] in same sp, *1 ch, 3 tr in next ch sp, 1 ch, [3 tr, 3 ch, 3 tr] in 3-ch corner sp, repeat from * twice more, 1 ch, 3 tr in next ch sp, 1 ch, join with ss to top of 3-ch. Fasten off. (36 tr, 8 1-ch sps, 4 3-ch sps)
Round 4: 1 ch, [dc, 3 ch, dc] in same sp, *2 ch, [dc in next ch sp, 2 ch] to corner 3-ch sp, [dc, 3 ch, dc] in 3-ch sp, repeat from * twice more, 2 ch, [dc in next ch sp, 2 ch] to end, join with ss to top of beginning dc. Fasten off. (16 dc, 12 2-ch sps, 4 3-ch sps)
Round 5: 3 ch, [2 tr, 3 ch, 3 tr] in same sp, *1 ch, [3 tr in next ch sp, 1 ch] to corner 3-ch sp, [3 tr, 3 ch, 3 tr] in corner 3-ch sp, repeat from * twice more, 1 ch, [3 tr in next ch sp, 1 ch] to end, join with ss to top of 3-ch. Fasten off. (60 tr, 16 1-ch sps, 4 3-ch sps)
Repeat Rounds 4 and 5 until desired size, changing colour every round.
