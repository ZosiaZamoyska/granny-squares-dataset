Make a magic ring.
Round 1: Ch 3, work 15 tr into ring, join with a sl st into top of beg ch-3.
Fasten off.
Round 2: Join new colour in any tr, ch 4, * work 1 tr into next tr, ch 1, rep from * around, join with a sl st into 3rd ch of beg ch-4.
Fasten off.
Round 3: Join new colour in any ch-1 sp, ch 3, work 1 tr into same sp, ch 1, * work 2 tr into next ch-1 sp, ch 1, rep from * around, join with a sl st into top of beg ch-3.
Fasten off.
Round 4: Join new colour in any ch-1 sp, ch 3, work 2 tr into same sp, ch 1, * work 3 tr into next ch-1 sp, ch 1, rep from * around, join with a sl st into top of beg ch-3.
Fasten off.
Round 5: Join new colour in any ch-1 sp, ch 4, work 2 dtr, ch 2, 3 dtr into same sp, ch 1, * work 3 tr into next ch-1 sp, ch 1, work 3 tr into next ch-1 sp, ch 1, work 3 tr into next ch-1 sp, ch 1, work 3 dtr, ch 2, 3 dtr into next ch-1 sp, ch 1, rep from * around, join with a sl st into top of beg ch-3.
Fasten off.
