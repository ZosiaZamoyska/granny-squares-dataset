ch– chain stitch
dc– double crochet
ch-sp – chain space
st– stitch
sl st– slip stitch for joining for different round.

magic ring
Round 1
ch3(count as dc), 2dc, ch4, [4dc, ch4] repeat repeat 3 times, slst to top of ch3.

Round 2
ch6, sk 3 st, 4dc ch4 4dc in corner space, ch2, [sk 3 st, 4dc ch4 4dc in corner space, ch2] repeat 2 times, sk 3 st, 4dc ch4 4dc in corner space, slst to top of ch3. 

Round 3
5ch(3ch as dc), sk 2 ch-sp, dc in next st, ch2, skip 3 st, 4dc ch4 4dc in corner sp, ch2, [sk 3 st, dc in next st, ch2, sk 2 ch-sp, dc in next st, ch2, sk 3 st, 4dc ch4 4dc in corner sp, ch2] repeat 3 times, slst to top of ch3. 

Round 4
5ch(3ch as dc), [sk 2 ch-sp, dc in next st, ch2] repeat 2 times, sk 3 st, 4dc ch4 4dc in corner sp, ch2, sk 3 st, dc in next st, [*(sk 2 ch-sp, dc in next st) repeat* 3 times, ch2, sk 3 st, 4dc ch4 4dc in corner sp, ch2, sk 3 st, dc in next st] repeat 3 times, ch2, slst to top of ch3, fasten off.
