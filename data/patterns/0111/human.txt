Make a magic ring.
Round 1: 1 sc and 7 half double crochet into the Magic Ring (8 stitches).
Round 2: From now on crochet only half double crochet stitches. [1 half double crochet, 4 half double crochet into the next stitch] repeat 4 times (20 stitches).
Round 3: 3 half double crochet, 4 half double crochet into the next stitch, [4 half double crochet, 4 half double crochet into the next stitch] repeat 3 times, 1 half double crochet (32 stitches).
Round 4: 5 half double crochet, 4 half double crochet into the next stitch, [7 half double crochet, 4 half double crochet into the next stitch] repeat 3 times, 2 half double crochet (44 stitches).
Round 5: 7 half double crochet, 4 half double crochet into the next stitch, [10 half double crochet, 4 half double crochet into the next stitch] repeat 3 times, 3 half double crochet (56 stitches).
Round 6: 9 half double crochet, 4 half double crochet into the next stitch, [13 half double crochet, 4 half double crochet into the next stitch] repeat 3 times, 4 half double crochet (68 stitches).
Round 7: If you want to crochet a larger coaster, skip this round and continue further down with round 8. Crochet only 1 sc and 1 slip stitch.
