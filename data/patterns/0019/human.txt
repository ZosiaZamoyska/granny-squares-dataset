ch- chain stitch
dc- double crochet
sp- space
Sl St– slip stitch for joining to different round
ch-sp- chain space
st– stitch

Magic loop, 
Round 1
with white yarn:
ch1+invisible standing st(count as dc)+2dc+ch1;
With blue yarn: ch1, fasten off white and weave in ends, 3dc+ch1;
With purple yarn: ch1, fasten off blue and weave in ends, 3dc+ch1;
With teal yarn: ch1, fasten off purple and weave in ends, 3dc+ch1;
With white yarn: ch1, sl st to the top of the first invisible standing st.

Round 2
Sl st back to the last ch-sp, ch1+invisible standing st(count as dc)+2dc+ch1, 3dc+ch1 in next ch-sp;
With blue yarn: ch1, fasten off white and weave in ends, 3dc+ch1 in the same ch-sp, 3dc+ch1 in next ch-sp;
With purple yarn: ch1, fasten off blue and weave in ends, 3dc+ch1 in the same ch-sp, 3dc+ch1 in next ch-sp;
With teal yarn: ch1, fasten off purple and weave in ends, 3dc+ch1 in the same ch-sp, 3dc+ch1 in next ch-sp;
With white yarn: ch1, sl st to the top of the first invisible standing st.

Round 3
Sl st back to the last corner, ch1+invisible standing st(count as dc)+2dc+ch1, 3dc+ch1 in next ch-sp, 3dc+ch1 in next corner;
With blue yarn: ch1, fasten off white and weave in ends, 3dc+ch1 in the same corner, 3dc+ch1 in next ch-sp, 3dc+ch1 in next corner;
With purple yarn: ch1, fasten off blue and weave in ends, 3dc+ch1 in the same corner, 3dc+ch1 in next ch-sp, 3dc+ch1 in next corner;
With teal yarn: ch1, fasten off purple and weave in ends, 3dc+ch1 in the same corner, 3dc+ch1 in next ch-sp, 3dc+ch1 in next corner;
With white yarn: ch1, sl st to the top of the first invisible standing st.

Round 4
Sl st back to the last corner, ch1+invisible standing st(count as dc)+2dc+ch1, *3dc+ch1 in next ch-sp* repeat 2 times, 3dc+ch1 in next corner;
With blue yarn: ch1, fasten off white and weave in ends, 3dc+ch1 in the same corner, *3dc+ch1 in next ch-sp* repeat 2 times, 3dc+ch1 in next corner;
With purple yarn: ch1, fasten off blue and weave in ends, 3dc+ch1 in the same corner, *3dc+ch1 in next ch-sp* repeat 2 times, 3dc+ch1 in next corner;
With teal yarn: ch1, fasten off purple and weave in ends, 3dc+ch1 in the same corner, *3dc+ch1 in next ch-sp* repeat 2 times, 3dc+ch1 in next corner;
With white yarn: ch1, sl st to the top of the first invisible standing st.

Round 5
Sl st back to the last corner, ch1+invisible standing st(count as dc)+2dc+ch1, *3dc+ch1 in next ch-sp* repeat 3 times, 3dc+ch1 in next corner;
With blue yarn: ch1, fasten off white and weave in ends, 3dc+ch1 in the same corner, *3dc+ch1 in next ch-sp* repeat 3 times, 3dc+ch1 in next corner;
With purple yarn: ch1, fasten off blue and weave in ends, 3dc+ch1 in the same corner, *3dc+ch1 in next ch-sp* repeat 3 times, 3dc+ch1 in next corner;
