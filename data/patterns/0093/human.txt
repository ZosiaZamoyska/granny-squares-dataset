Make a magic ring.

Round 1: ch 3 (counts as 1 dc), 1 dc, ch 1, [2 dc, ch1] 5 times, join with sl st in top of ch 3, fasten off
You should have 6 groups of 2 dc with ch 1 spaces in between.

Round 2: In each ch 1 space [2 dc, ch 1, 2 dc, ch 1], join with sl st in first dc, fasten off

Round 3: In each ch 1 space [3 dc, ch1], join with sl st in first dc, fasten off

Round 4: In each ch 1 space [2 dc, ch 1, 2 dc, ch1], join with sl st in first dc, fasten off

Round 5: In each ch 1 space [2 dc, ch1], join with sl st in first dc, fasten off

Round 6: In each ch 1 space [3 dc, ch 1], join with sl st in first dc, fasten off

Round 7: In each ch 1 space [1 sc, ch 3], join with a sl st in first sc, don't fasten off

Round 8: Sl st into ch 3 space, ch 1, 3 sc, ch1...
* in next ch 3 space, [2 sc, 1 hdc, ch 1]...
in next ch 3 space, [1 hdc, 2 dc, ch 1]...
in next ch 3 space, [3 tr, ch 2, 3 tr, ch 1]... (corner made)
in next ch 3 space, [2 dc, 1 hdc, ch 1]...
in next ch 3 space, [1 hdc, 2 sc, ch1]...
in next ch 3 space, [3 sc, ch 1]...
repeat from * around, join with sl st in first sc, don't fasten off

Round 9: sl st in next two sc, sl st in next ch 1 space...
ch 3, 2 dc, ch 1
in each ch 1 space [3 dc, ch 1], and in corner spaces [3 dc, ch 2, 3 dc, ch 1]... sl st in first dc, fasten off!!
