st/sts: stitch/stitches
ch: chain
ch-2-sp: chain 2 space (which will be the corner space)
dc: double crochet
sl st: slip stitch
Pattern Notes:

Make a magic ring.
Round 1: Ch 3. Into the ring, work 1 dc, ch 2. Into ring, work (3 dc, ch 2) three times. Work 1 dc, and join with sl st into the beginning chain. Sl st into the ch-2-sp [12 sts, 4 ch 2 spaces]
Round 2: Ch 3. Work (1 dc, 2 ch, 2dc) in the next ch-2-sp. Make *1 dc in each dc along the side, make (2 dc, ch 2, 2 dc) in ch-2-space. Repeat for the next two sides. Dc in the dc on the last side. Make 1 dc into each of the sl sts from the previous round. Sl st into the ch-2-sp. [28 sts, 4 ch-2-spaces]
Round 3: Repeat round 2 [44 sts, 4 ch-2-spaces]
Round 4: Repeat round 2 [60 sts, 4 ch-2-spaces]
Round 5: Repeat round 2 [76 sts, 4 ch-2-spaces]
Round 6: Repeat round 2 [92 sts, 4 ch-2-spaces]
Round 7: Repeat round 2 [108 sts, 4 ch-2-spaces]
