
Start with a magic loop
Round 1
ch3 as standing st, 2dc, ch2, 3dc, ch1. Add new color yarn by yo and pull the new yarn through the loop, 3dc, ch2, 3dc, ch1, pull up tightly, hdc to join the top of first ch3.

Round 2
Flip the workpiece to work on the wrong side, ch3 as standing st, dc in the same corner space, dc in the following 3 sts, 2dc+ch2+2dc in the next corner space, dc in the next 3 sts, 2dc+ch2 in the next corner space. Switch the yarn, 2dc in the same corner space, dc in the following 3 sts, 2dc+ch2+2dc in the next corner space, dc in the next 3 sts, 2dc in the next corner space, hdc to join the top of first ch3.

Round 3
Flip the workpiece to work on the right side, ch3 as standing st, dc in the same corner space, dc in the following 7 sts, 2dc+ch2+2dc in the next corner space, dc in the next 7 sts, 2dc+ch2 in the next corner space. Switch the yarn, 2dc in the same corner space, dc in the following 7 sts, 2dc+ch2+2dc in the next corner space, dc in the next 7 sts, 2dc in the last corner space, hdc to join the top of first ch3.

Round 4
Flip the workpiece to work on the wrong side, ch3 as standing st, dc in the same corner space, dc in the following 11 sts, 2dc+ch2+2dc in the next corner space, dc in the next 11 sts, 2dc+ch2 in the next corner space. Switch the yarn, 2dc in the same corner space, dc in the following 11 sts, 2dc+ch2+2dc in the next corner space, dc in the following 11 sts, 2dc+ch2 in the next corner space, sl st to the top of first ch3.
