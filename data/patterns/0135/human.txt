Make a magic ring.
Round 1: Ch 3, 15 d c in ring, join with sl st to 3rd st of ch-3 first made.
Round 2: Ch 4, d c in each d c with ch-1 between d c's. Join last ch 1 with sl st to 3rd st of ch-4 (16 sps).
Round 3: Ch 5, d c in next sp, * ch 4, skip 1 sp, 2 d c with ch-2 between in next sp, repeat from * around, ending with ch 4 and sl st to 3rd st of ch-5 first made.
Round 4: Ch 3, 4 d c in next sp, 5 d c in each of next 2 sps, * ch 13, skip 1 sp, 5 d c in each of next 3 sps, repeat from * 2 more times, then ch 13 and join with sl st to 3rd st of ch-3 first made. Break off.
